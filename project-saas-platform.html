PROJECT_002

DeepReach SaaS Platform

A large-scale social intelligence system that analyzes accounts, reels, trends, and audience signals to deliver real actionable analytics for growth.

Client: Clipping Startup
Duration: 4 months
Status: Online

// PROJECT OVERVIEW

We built DeepReach SaaS Platform to solve one problem: finding real growth signals from social media at scale. Manual research is slow, inconsistent, and based on guesses. Influencers, brands, and creators spend hours scrolling without any real direction.

DeepReach automates the entire process. It analyzes thousands of accounts , reels, posts, and engagement profiles, and converts them into structured insights. It detects trends early, evaluates view patterns, and reveals which content formats, hooks, and creators actually drive performance

The system runs continuously and updates datasets in real time. Teams get reliable analytics instead of assumptions, and can plan content, reach, and distribution with confidence.
