// PROJECT OVERVIEW

We developed an advanced high-frequency trading system that automates execution, risk management, and strategy logic. The agent operates in real-time, scanning market conditions, reacting to micro price changes, and placing orders at speeds no human trader can match.

Traditional manual trading suffered from delayed reactions, emotional decisions, and inconsistent execution. The objective was clear: remove human limitations and build a fully automated trading engine capable of continuous operation.
